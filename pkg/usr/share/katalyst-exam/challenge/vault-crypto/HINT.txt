Hint: look closely at _derive_seed() in encrypt.py.

It hashes the key with SHA-256 (a 256-bit hash) but the LCG seed it
produces is much smaller than 256 bits. How small? That's the key
question -- once you know the real size of the seed space, a brute
force over every possible seed becomes very fast.

There's no flag marker in the decrypted output. You'll need a way to
automatically tell a correct decryption apart from a wrong one (think
about what the plaintext should look like as bytes).
